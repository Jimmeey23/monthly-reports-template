# SUPREME HQ BANDRA — JULY 2026 STRATEGIC REVIEW

## Speaker Script

### OPENING

Good morning, everyone.

We are operating from a position of serious financial strength.

July delivered excellent top-line revenue, driven by high-value, price-resilient clients. The demand is clearly there.

Now, our focus shifts to tightening our operational mechanics — speeding up our sales cycle, pruning dead weight on the schedule, and plugging a very solvable retention leak — to turn a great quarter into a record-breaking one.

# 01 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Good morning, everyone. Let's kick things off with the headline: July was a fantastic month for the balance sheet. We closed just shy of ₹18 Lakhs in net revenue, blowing past our June numbers and our 2026 averages.

People are coming in, and they are buying. The only slight headwind is our churn rate at 26.8%, which means we're working a bit too hard at the top of the funnel to replace natural attrition.

But as you'll see in the data today, this isn't a mystery we have to solve — it's just a process we need to tighten. We have the playbook ready.

# 02 — COMMERCIAL REVENUE PERFORMANCE

Looking at our revenue mix, we are clearly attracting a premium buyer. We had slightly fewer unique buyers this month, but our average ticket size jumped 13%. We're monetizing every mat at a much higher rate.

We also saw a massive surge in class packages. It's a great cash-flow injection for the month, but it does mean we need to be proactive about re-engaging these folks when their packs run out.

I also want to highlight a massive operational win: the front desk actively steered clients toward fee-free payment methods. That one behavioral shift saved us nearly ₹50K in processing fees. That's pure margin, right to the bottom line.

# 03 — ACQUISITION FUNNEL PERFORMANCE

Marketing is doing their job — the top of the funnel is absolutely packed with a record 220 leads. But right now, our sales engine is idling a bit too long.

Our average time to close a lead has stretched to 45 days. In boutique fitness, intent decays in hours, not weeks. If we don't close them while the post-workout endorphins are still high, we lose them. We are instituting a strict 48-hour follow-up rule starting this week.

Also, a quick note on marketing spend: our hosted pop-ups look great for brand awareness, but they are bringing in free-trial seekers, not buyers. We're going to pivot that energy back toward high-intent channels like WhatsApp and client referrals, which are converting beautifully.

# 04 — DELIVERY, FORMATS & INSTRUCTOR PERFORMANCE

Floor economics are looking stellar. Because of our shift to packages, our revenue per visit is up nearly 60%. When people show up, they've paid well to be there.

Our PowerCycle classes, especially with instructors like Atulan, are practically waitlist-only. But we have a payroll leak we need to fix: 16 classes ran to empty rooms this month. We are going to aggressively prune those underperforming slots.

More importantly, we have a glaring opportunity. Our flagship location sells out 'Strength Lab' classes at 89% capacity, and Bandra currently offers zero Strength classes. We are going to import that flagship playbook immediately and swap our empty classes for Strength Lab.

# 05 — RETENTION & CHURN DYNAMICS

Retention is where we can pull our biggest levers this quarter. The data shows us exactly when a client decides to leave us: it's usually right around class number three.

If they don't build a habit in the first 14 days, they become a flight risk. So, we're launching a 'Quick Starter' milestone to reward early consistency. Get them over the hump, and they stay.

We also noticed that retention starts at the sales desk. Akshay's clients renew at 91%, while other associates are seeing renewals in the 50s. We are going to record Akshay's consults, codify his playbook, and train the rest of the team to sell the habit, not just the discount.

# 06 — STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

To wrap this up: we don't need a massive capital injection or a total strategy overhaul to have a record-breaking August. We just need operational discipline.

We are making five targeted moves: We're launching Strength Lab to fill empty rooms. We're putting our best closers on the prime trial slots. We're forcing a 48-hour follow-up on every lead. We're deploying a strategic 10% save-offer for at-risk accounts, and we're cutting the dead weight off the schedule.

# 07 — FORWARD OUTLOOK & SCENARIO PLANNING

If we execute these five levers, and they cost us practically nothing to implement, we push our August run-rate past the **₹20 Lakh** mark.

The demand is there, the brand is strong, and now the operations will match.

# CLOSING

Let's get to work.
